## 1. The service
Hermia translates phone calls live. When you decline or miss a call, your carrier forwards it to our
service number; we ring you in the app and translate both sides in real time. You keep your own number and SIM.

## 2. Your account
- You sign in with email or Apple, then confirm your own phone number by calling our service number from it (or
  by an SMS code). One account per number.
- You are responsible for activity on your account and for keeping your phone secure.

## 3. Call forwarding and carrier charges
- You set up conditional call forwarding on your phone to our service number. Your carrier may charge you for
  forwarded calls under your plan; we don't control or refund those charges.
- Forwarding depends on your carrier. Some carriers or phone settings (for example, iPhone Call Screening)
  stop it from working.
- Turn forwarding off in the app before you stop using Hermia, or calls will keep going to our number.

## 4. Translation is automatic and can be wrong
- Translations are made by machine and **can contain errors**, especially numbers, prices, dates, addresses and
  names. Confirm anything important (amounts, appointments, medical or legal details) another way.
- **Do not use Hermia for emergency calls.** It does not connect to 911 or any emergency service.
- The people who call you hear a notice that the call is translated by software. Don't use the service to
  mislead anyone or to record anyone without consent.

## 5. Plans, minutes and payment
- Plans include a number of translated minutes per billing period. Minutes count from when you answer the
  translated call. Calls placed from our number on your behalf (callbacks) may count at a higher rate, shown
  in the app. Unused minutes don't carry over.
- When your minutes run out, new calls aren't translated and a call in progress ends; callers hear a short
  message.
- Subscriptions are bought and billed through Apple's App Store under Apple's terms. Manage or cancel them in
  your Apple account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple.
- We may change plans or prices with notice; changes apply from your next billing period.

## 6. Acceptable use
Don't use Hermia to break the law, harass people, send spam or robocalls, get around call blocking, or
interfere with the service. We may suspend accounts that do.

## 7. Privacy
Our Privacy Policy explains what we process. We don't record or store call audio or transcripts.

## 8. Availability
We aim for reliable service but don't guarantee it will be uninterrupted or error-free. Calls can drop, ring
late or go untranslated because of networks, carriers or providers.
